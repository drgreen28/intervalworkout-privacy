# IntervalWorkout Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 3, 2026

IntervalWorkout ("the app") is an interval timer for workouts. This policy explains what
information the app handles and what choices you have. It applies to the app on Android.

## The short version

- Your timers, workout history and settings stay **on your device**. We do not run accounts or
  servers, and we do not receive them.
- The app has **no analytics and no crash-reporting service**.
- The app shows ads from **Google AdMob**, and offers a one-time purchase to remove them, handled
  by **Google Play**. Those two Google services are the only places information about you leaves
  your device because of the app.
- You can change your advertising choices at any time, and you can remove ads entirely.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores the following on your device only:

- the timers you create (names, intervals, repeat counts)
- a record of workouts you complete (date, time trained, sets done)
- your settings (sound and vibration, theme, text size, workout behaviour)
- whether you have purchased "Remove ads"

We do not receive any of this. It is deleted when you uninstall the app. You can also clear your
workout history from **Options → Reset progress**.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads on some screens, provided by Google AdMob. Ads are never shown while a
workout is running.

To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud and abuse, Google and its partners may collect and
use information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate
your general location), device and app information, diagnostic and performance information, and
how you interact with ads. Where you have given consent, this may include
personalizing the ads you see. We do not receive this information.

Learn how Google handles it:

- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## Your choices

- **Consent.** Where the law requires it, including the European Economic Area and the United
  Kingdom, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent form before showing ads that use
  your data. You can review or change your choice at any time in **Options → Advertising privacy
  choices**, which appears wherever it applies to you.
- **Device settings.** You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in
  Android **Settings → Privacy → Ads**.
- **Remove ads.** Buying "Remove ads" removes every ad, and the app then makes no ad requests.

## Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your
payment details. The app asks Google Play whether you own the purchase, when it starts and when
you choose **Restore purchases**, so that ads stay removed on your devices.

## Permissions

The app requests these Android permissions, for these reasons only:

- **Internet and network state:** to load ads and to check your purchase. The timer, workouts,
  history and statistics all work offline.
- **Notifications and foreground service:** to show the running-workout notification and keep the
  timer accurate while the screen is off.
- **Wake lock:** to keep the screen on during a workout if you choose that option.
- **Vibration:** for the vibration cues, if you turn them on.
- **Advertising ID:** used by the ad service described above.
- **Billing:** to make and check the "Remove ads" purchase.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from
children.

## Retention and deletion

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Uninstalling
the app removes everything it stores on your device. For information held by Google, see the
links above.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or US state privacy laws),
you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, and to object to or withdraw
consent for certain processing. Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, requests
about advertising data are for Google. If you have any question about this policy, contact us.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page, and the effective date
above shows when it last changed.
